# Parish Admin Training Manual

This is for a priest or secretary who looks after a parish's information in the CatholicSA app. It only covers things that already work in the app today - nothing in here is a promise of a future feature.

## 1. Signing in

Open the app (https://catholicsa.co.za) and sign in with your email and password, or with Google, from the **Account** tab. If you don't have an account yet, create one from the same screen.

## 2. Becoming an admin of your parish

Being an admin of a parish is what lets you edit that parish's information. You can't grant this to yourself - someone who is already a Super Admin has to add you, once, by searching for your name or email on the **Users** screen and assigning you as an admin of your parish.

Once that's done, open the **Account** tab. You'll see a banner: "You're a parish admin for [your parish's name]," with an **Admin Panel** button. If you administer more than one parish, you'll see one banner per parish.

## 3. Adding a church

From your parish's **Admin Panel**:

1. Tap **Add Church**.
2. Fill in the church's name, address, phone/email (optional), and building type (Church, Cathedral, Basilica, Convent, or Chapel).
3. Tap **Add Church** to save it.

That's enough to get it listed and start adding Mass times. GPS location and photos are meant to be added once you've actually visited - see the next step.

## 4. Setting the real GPS location

Do this standing at (or right outside) the church itself:

1. Open that church in the Admin Panel.
2. Under Church Verification, tap **"I'm at the church - use this location."**
3. Allow location access if your device asks. That's it - the real coordinates are saved.

If you're not there yet, there's a separate **"Mark GPS 'add later'"** button, so the church isn't stuck waiting on this one thing.

## 5. Adding photos

From the church's page in the Admin Panel, use the photo upload button to add pictures of the building. A caption is optional.

## 6. Hiding a church while you're still confirming it

Every church you add or edit shows up on the public map immediately, by default. If you'd rather finish checking it in person first - correct address, real GPS, a real photo, confirmed Mass times - before anyone sees it:

- In that church's page in the Admin Panel, switch **"Visible on public map"** off. It stays fully visible and editable to you; it just won't show to the public yet. Switch it back on once you're happy with it.
- A Super Admin can also hide *every* church at once (Super Admin → System → Churches → "Hide All Churches From Public") - useful when a whole parish's worth of churches needs re-checking in person before anyone sees them, like during an initial data clean-up. Parishes themselves stay visible either way; only individual church buildings are affected.

## 7. Adding Mass times

From the church's page in the Admin Panel, use the Mass times editor to add a date, time, type (Mass or Communion Service), and language. These are entered week by week, not as a single recurring template, since which priest and which time can genuinely change week to week.

## 8. Adding an event

From the Admin Panel, use **Add Event** to give it a title, description, date, time, and category. It shows up automatically in the app's **Events** tab once saved.

## 9. Verifying a church

Once a church has a confirmed Mass time, at least one admin, GPS set (or explicitly marked "add later"), and its associations reviewed, a priest (not a secretary) can mark it **Verified** from the same Church Verification section. This is a deliberate, real confirmation, not a formality - it's not available until those conditions are actually met.

## Questions or something not working?

If something in here doesn't match what you see in the app, say so - this manual gets corrected, not the other way around.
